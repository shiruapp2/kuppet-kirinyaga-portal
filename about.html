Our Vision

To build a vibrant, transparent, and accountable KUPPET Kirinyaga Branch that safeguards teachers’ welfare, promotes professional growth, and fosters unity, justice, service, and solidarity among all members and with the National KUPPET, advancing a progressive national teachers’ union founded on cohesion, reconciliation, and collective empowerment.

Our Mission

To champion teachers’ rights and welfare through transparent resource management, effective communication, and responsive service delivery, while promoting unity, ethical leadership, job security, professional growth, and fair resolution of members’ grievances through lawful union activities, affiliation, collective bargaining, and research.

KUPPET Constitution & Rules

Explore the guiding principles, structures, and regulations of the Kenya Union of Post Primary Education Teachers.

Articles 1.0 - 3.0: Preamble, Interpretation, Name & Motto

ARTICLE 1.0: PREAMBLE

The trade union constituted by this constitution shall be called the KENYA UNION OF POST PRIMARY EDUCATION TEACHERS (KUPPET), with the headquarters in Nairobi.

ARTICLE 1.0.1: INTERPRETATION

  • “Delegate” means registered officials and members of KUPPET...
  • “Member” means a teacher/lecturer/trainer who is a fully paid up member.

ARTICLE 2.0: NAME

The organisation shall be called; Kenya Union of Post-Primary Education Teachers (KUPPET)...

ARTICLE 3.0: MOTTO

Service, Justice and Solidarity.

Articles 4.0 - 6.0: Aims, Mission & Vision

ARTICLE 4.0: THE AIMS/OBJECTIVES

  • To advocate for the establishment of sound, ethical and professional policies...
  • To make every endeavor to organise all the teachers...
  • To promote, improve and develop ethical principles...

ARTICLE 5.0: MISSION

To unite all teachers in post primary institutions in Kenya...

ARTICLE 6.0: VISION

To be a strong National progressive post primary teachers’ union...

Article 7.0: Membership

ARTICLE 7.0: MEMBERSHIP

  • Teachers/ Trainers in secondary and tertiary institutions of good conduct...
  • Entry membership fee of KShs.50...
Article 8.0: Organs of the Union

8.0 There is established the organs of the UNION namely:

  • Annual Delegates’ Conference (ADC)
  • National Advisory Council (NAC)
  • National Governing Council (NGC)
  • National Executive Board (NEB)
