KUPPET Constitution & Rules (Complete Document)

Articles 1.0 - 3.0: Preamble, Interpretation, Name & Motto

ARTICLE 1.0: PREAMBLE

The trade union constituted by this constitution shall be called the KENYA UNION OF POST PRIMARY EDUCATION TEACHERS (KUPPET), with the headquarters in Nairobi.

WHEREAS the Kenya Union of Post Primary Education Teachers has been founded upon principles of social justice and professionalism and is dedicated to the achievement of greater unity among her members for the purpose of improving their standards of living, bringing justice and dignity at the workplace and securing social equity for all;

WHEREAS the union has formulated and adopted the following rules to regulate relationship between members and between members and employers and to organise collective bargaining for the purposes of meeting her objectives;

AND WHEREAS to these ends the KENYA UNION OF POST-PRIMARY EDUCATION TEACHERS shall be an honourable institution in which membership shall be a source of pride.

ARTICLE 1.0.1: INTERPRETATION

  • “Delegate” means registered officials and members of KUPPET entitled to attend the Annual and Special Delegates Conferences.
  • “Member” means a teacher/lecturer/trainer who is a fully paid up member of KUPPET.
  • “Trade Union” means Kenya Union of Post Primary Education Teachers or the meaning assigned to it under Labour Relations Act, 2007.
  • “County” is a geographical location within which KUPPET branch is confined.

ARTICLE 2.0: NAME

The organisation shall be called; Kenya Union of Post-Primary Education Teachers (KUPPET), hereinafter referred to as the UNION. The registered office of the union shall be Lower Hill Duplex Apartment, Lower Hill Road, Suite 51/P. O. Box 30412-00100, NAIROBI or as may be determined from time to time by the Union.

ARTICLE 3.0: MOTTO

Service, Justice and Solidarity.

Articles 4.0 - 6.0: Aims/Objectives, Mission & Vision

ARTICLE 4.0: THE AIMS/OBJECTIVES

  • To advocate for the establishment of sound, ethical and professional policies that guarantee job security and fair solution to the members’ grievances.
  • To make every endeavor to organise all the teachers in the post primary institutions and to ensure absolute unity of the members for effective and efficient representation.
  • To promote, improve and develop ethical principles to inculcate responsibility among the teachers to guarantee their health, professional, social and economic well-being.
  • To develop the capacity of members through seminars, symposiums and workshops locally and internationally.
  • To organise lawful processions or demonstrations to promote the members’ freedom of expression and collective rights.
  • To participate in all matters aimed at providing quality education and formulation of sound policies towards common system of education.
  • To make every endeavour to promote effective representation locally, nationally and internationally in collaboration with such other related organisations and/or agencies to protect the teachers’ interest and education generally.
  • To negotiate with the government of Kenya/its agencies, any public or private institutions offering education or related services better terms and conditions of employment and thereby formulate clear representation and negotiation procedures including promotion and its packages.
  • To join or to be joined in any research by the government of Kenya (its agency / department / Ministry) any public or private organisation/ institution on matters related to teaching as a profession.
  • (i) To co-operate in or take part in research together with other societies, bodies, associations, unions or organisations within or outside the country having similar aims and objectives. (ii) To engage in/take part in education related projects with other societies/ bodies/organisations within and outside the country.
  • To promote and protect the rights of teachers serving in education industry.
  • To articulate the members’ interests in the formulation of education ordinances, policies and propagate the same to the members for smooth administration and dispensation of education.
  • To offer assistance and encourage teachers to form saving and credit societies, Burial and Benevolent Funds, academic institutions, land buying companies and/or any amenities.
  • To acquire any deeds, loans or “by purchase or lease” any movable or immovable objects.
  • To seek and obtain legal advice/protection on matters affecting the union and member(s) whether directly or indirectly arising out of relations between the union /members and the employer(s) to improve the members’ social and economic status.
  • To establish KUPPET branch offices based on County demarcations.
  • To establish at National level sub-committees such as: finance and administration committee, constitution and by-laws committee, industrial relations and research committee, and any other committees decided upon by the delegates conference.
  • To affiliate to workers organisations locally/internationally for a united front to champion the rights of Teachers and promote education for all.
  • To sensitise members on HIV/AIDS/Drugs/Substance abuse at the work place.
  • To advocate against child labour/abuse/trafficking.
  • To provide a conducive environment for arbitration of disputes at workplace and to liaise with other organisations/agencies to enhance cohesion and conciliation at the workplace.
  • To create/advocate for gender equity and mainstreaming at the workplace.
  • To buttress gender equity and equal opportunities without any form of discrimination on basis of, inter alia, sex, race, tribe, age or religion.

ARTICLE 5.0: MISSION

To unite all teachers in post primary institutions in Kenya by advocating for the establishment of sound ethical and professional policies that guarantee job security and fair solutions to members’ grievances through lawful union activities, affiliation, negotiation and research.

ARTICLE 6.0: VISION

To be a strong National progressive post primary teachers’ union in Kenya where justice, service and integrity are upheld to enhance cohesion and conciliation.

Article 7.0: Membership

ARTICLE 7.0: MEMBERSHIP

  • (i) Teachers/ Trainers in secondary and tertiary institutions of good conduct and reputation, certified, licensed or authorised to teach, shall have the right of admission to any of the branches of the Union upon payment of the entrance fee as provided in the rules of the union.
  • A member may retain his membership in the Union even after retirement or resignation from the service of TSC or any relevant Teachers employer provided that he or she is fully subscribed.
  • If in the opinion of any Branch the applicant’s professional conduct makes it undesirable that his/her name should not be on the Roll of Members, the matter shall be referred to the National Governing Council for enquiry by the Professional and Standards Committee of the union.
  • (i) Upon admission, the teacher’s full name shall be written in the Union’s register showing the member’s name in the Teachers’ Register kept by the Teachers Service Commission and where possible the name of school/institution to which the member is assigned at the time of admission.
  • The member shall be provided with membership card bearing his/her name in full, address and such further particulars that may be necessary to afford easy identification of the member.
  • No person shall be admitted to the union or to any of its Branches, unless he/she previously agrees to abide by the rules of the union contained in this constitution.
  • (i) Entry membership fee of KShs.50 (fifty shillings only) to the Union shall be paid once and monthly subscription fee for all members shall be at a rate of 1.8% (one point eight per cent) of a member’s basic pay.
  • Monthly subscription shall in as far as possible be collected through the check-off system.
  • If at any time the National Governing Council is of the opinion that the allocation of the subscription should be revised they shall seek authority from National Delegates Conference.
  • KUPPET shall operate on a closed shop system.
  • Any member of a Branch of the union who is transferred from one County branch to another, shall on payment of all dues, be entitled to a “DEEM IT” which shall make him/her eligible for enrolment in the branch.
  • Every member shall be at liberty to withdraw his/her membership from the union by clearly addressing his/her resignation to the employer and a copy to the Branch Executive Secretary in which case the party withdrawing will cease to be entitled to enjoy any benefits or privileges of the union or any of its Branches. Under no circumstances shall any subscriptions or other donations paid to the union by a withdrawing member be refunded.
  • Honorary/Associate Membership may be conferred on any person who in the opinion of the Delegates Conference has rendered outstanding service to the cause of the Union/Education or other persons not eligible for ordinary membership. Such honorary membership shall not carry any right to vote on any matter.
  • No Honorary Member shall be eligible to hold any office in the union.
  • Any teacher having been declared ineligible by the National Governing Council (NGC) shall remain ineligible until such a time as the Delegates Conference may decide to admit his/her membership.
  • Associate/honorary members shall not be obliged to pay any fees or subscription.
  • Members of the Union shall be at liberty to join other Educational Organisations, so long as the rules and regulations of such organisations are not contrary to the rules and regulations of Kenya Union of Post-Primary Education Teachers (KUPPET).
Article 8.0 - 8.3: Organs of the Union & Delegates Conference

8.0 ORGANS OF THE UNION

There is established the organs of the UNION namely:

  • 1. Annual Delegates’ Conference (ADC)
  • 2. National Advisory Council (NAC)
  • 3. National Governing Council (NGC)
  • 4. National Executive Board (NEB)
  • 5. Regional Council (RC)
  • 6. Branch General Assembly (BGA)
  • 7. Branch Governing Council (BGC)
  • 8. Branch Executive Committee (BEC)
  • 9. Trustees
  • 10. School/Institutions Representative (SR/IR)

8.1.0 The Annual Delegates’ Conference (ADC)

  • There shall be convened annually in the month of November/December, at such date and place as may be fixed by National Governing Council (NGC) an annual delegates’ conference of the UNION.
  • The notice to the Annual Delegates’ Conference shall be issued by the Secretary General as directed by the National Governing Council.
  • The notice regarding the Annual Delegates’ Conference; while being issued, shall be within four weeks before the conference.
  • The National Governing Council (NGC) may convene a Special Delegates’ Conference whenever necessary provided that not less than two weeks’ notice is given. Such conference shall have similar powers and functions as the Annual Delegates’ Conference.
  • Unless expressly provided for, the Delegates’ Conferences for the purposes of elections shall be on the 5th year not later than the second week of December following KUPPET National elections.

8.2.0 Delegates to the Conference

  • The delegates attending the conference shall be: Members of the National Executive Board, the members of the National Governing Council, Trustees and Delegates from the 47 county Branches.
  • (i) Each county Branch shall send fourteen (14) delegates being representative of between 1-300 members to the Annual Delegates’ Conference, all of whom must be bona-fide registered officials of the Branch.
  • Delegates duly appointed at the Branch General Assembly being one delegate for every one hundred (100) over and above the 300 bona fide members registered in the membership register at such Branch shall attend the Annual Delegates Conference.
  • The National Executive Board may call for a Special Delegates Conference for critical decision-making in which at least three delegates from each Branch shall be invited to attend.
  • Any replacement of the Delegates must be approved by the Branch Governing Council. A list of replacement must be sent to the Secretary General fourteen days to the conference.
  • The Secretary General shall avail the replacement lists thereof to the National Executive Board a week to the conference. The quorum to the conference shall be a third (1/3) of delegates to attend.
  • Only members of the National Executive Board and Branch Executive Committees qualify to contest at the national elections.

8.3.0 The Functions of the Delegates’ Conference

  • The Delegates’ Conference shall be the supreme authority of the UNION and its decisions shall be final and binding on all members.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall adopt financial statements/reports.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall approve the budget proposal presented by NGC for the following year.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall determine the broad basic policies and programs of the Union for the future.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall delegate powers to the National Executive Board for the purposes of setting up specialised departments and other advisory bodies at the Secretariat to carry out the aims and objectives of the Union and disciplinary issues.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall deal with educational matters affecting the union and the Republic of Kenya.
  • (i) The Delegates’ Conference shall consider all matters related to membership and discipline.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall ratify decisions of the National Governing Council.
  • The Delegates’ Conference shall delegate to the National Governing Council powers to discipline a member/branch that breaches the KUPPET regulations.
  • Shall consider amendments of the Union Constitution.
Articles 8.4 - 8.7: NAC, NGC, NEB & Functions

8.4.0 National Advisory Council (NAC)

  • There shall be established a National Advisory Council (NAC) which shall comprise elected national officials; the National Chairperson, the National Vice Chairperson, The Secretary General, The Deputy Secretary General, National Treasurer, Assistant National Treasurer, National Secretary Gender, National Secretary Tertiary, National Secretary Secondary, National Organising Secretary, Branch Chairpersons, Branch secretaries and Branch Treasurers from all the counties.
  • The council shall be chaired by the Union's National chairperson.
  • The secretary to the National advisory council shall be the Secretary General.
  • Shall meet once a year. Shall meet as and when the National Executive Board so determines. Exercise their role collectively and not individually.

Functions of National Advisory Council:

  • Render advice to the National Governing Council on selected union matters.
  • Advise on the Unions mission, vision, and purpose and core values.
  • Advise on the strategic direction and significant policies of the Union.
  • Ensure that the strategy of the Union is aligned to the purpose of the organisation and the legitimate interests and expectations of the members and her stakeholders.
  • Advise on the Organisational structure.

8.5.0 National Governing Council (NGC)

  • The NGC shall comprise of the Elected National Officials namely the National Chairperson, the National Vice Chairperson, the Secretary General, the Deputy Secretary General, National Treasurer, Assistant National Treasurer, National Secretary Gender, National Secretary Tertiary, National Secretary Secondary, National Organising Secretary, 1ST Assistant National Gender Secretary, 2ND Assistant National Gender Secretary, 3RD Assistant National Gender Secretary and Assistant National Secretary Junior Secondary, together with Branch Secretaries from the counties and sixteen (16) nominee women representatives.
  • The quorum shall be a third (1/3) of members expected to attend.

8.5.1 The Functions of the National Governing Council (NGC)

  • Shall meet twice a year. Shall meet as and when the National Executive Board so determines. Shall meet within 14 days if 1/3 of the National Governing Council signs a requisition for a meeting.
  • Shall ensure that the business of the Union is properly transacted between the Annual Delegates’ Conferences.
  • Shall ensure that the decisions and policies of the Delegates’ Conference are fully executed.
  • Shall enforce decisions of the union as determined/passed by Delegates’ Conference.
  • Shall ensure adherence to the Constitution, rules and regulations, standing orders, by-laws of the union and take such steps as may be deemed necessary for such purposes.
  • Shall discipline a Member/Branch of the Union.
  • Shall solicit funds through loans, advances, overdrafts and mortgage for Union projects.
  • Shall recommend removal of any official(s) from its meetings for a period not exceeding one year for gross misconduct/having acted contrary to the union constitution after two consecutive warnings.
  • Shall develop by-laws at National and branch levels.
  • Shall exclude from the Union any branch which in their opinion has failed to comply with the rules of the union.
  • Shall recommend removal from office any officers for gross misconduct/ flouting of the constitution/rules provided that 2/3 of members endorse.
  • Shall direct the Deputy Secretary General to issue notice of meetings of the National Governing Council, National Advisory Council and the National Delegates Conference if the Secretary General/any other officer acting on his/her instruction fails to issue notice or is incapacitated in any way provided two thirds requisition of members endorse.
  • The National Governing Council (NGC) shall determine the allowances for the Trustees.
  • Shall prepare by-laws to run gratuity and benevolent funds.
  • Shall nominate twelve (12) women representatives to the National Governing Council considering regional balance.
  • Set and oversee the overall strategy and approves significant policies of the Union. Monitor the Union’s performance.

8.6.0 The National Executive Board (NEB)

  • There shall be established the National Executive Board (NEB) comprising fourteen elected national officials who shall reflect regional and gender balance, and include the representation of persons abled differently and Junior Secondary School. The National Officials include: The National Chairperson, the National Vice Chairperson, the Secretary General, the Deputy Secretary General, the National Treasurer, Assistant National Treasurer, National Secretary Gender, National Secretary Tertiary, National Secretary Secondary, National Organising Secretary, 1ST Assistant National Gender Secretary, 2ND Assistant National Gender Secretary, 3RD Assistant National Gender Secretary and Assistant National Secretary Junior Secondary.
  • The members of National Executive Board shall work on full time basis provided that any member who gets engaged as a state officer shall serve the union on part-time basis.
  • Notwithstanding the provision above, National Executive Board may prescribe such other terms for members of the board who are serving as state officers.
  • This organ shall be based at the secretariat and shall have mandate to transact the business of the union at the headquarters.
  • The National Executive Board and the other national office employees shall form the secretariat staff.

8.7.0 Functions of the National Executive Board

  • The National Executive Board (NEB) shall be responsible for the day-to-day affairs of the union.
  • Shall exercise delegated authority on behalf of the Delegate’s Conference, the National Advisory Council and National Governing Council.
  • Shall conduct meetings at least once each month or as may be necessary.
  • Shall exercise authority/mandate to negotiate/enter into Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) with the Government/Employer/any other authority on behalf of the members.
  • Shall implement the policies of the Union as outlined in the aims and objectives of the union under the direction of Delegates’ Conference.
  • Shall make decisions binding the Union in circumstances of emergencies/ when National Governing Council has not met/when necessary.
  • Shall recruit and determine terms of service for secretariat staff.
  • Shall interpret rules and regulations between meetings of the Delegates’ Conferences.
  • Shall determine terms of reference for various union committees and honoraria for the committee members, shall maintain the union finances and submit a report and a statement of accounts to the Annual Delegates’ Conference of the Union.
  • Shall set up specialised departments and other advisory bodies at the Secretariat to carry out the aims and objectives of the Union and disciplinary issues.
  • Shall maintain the union projects at National levels.
  • Shall initiate and discipline a member or branch of the Union.
  • Shall enforce the decision of the Union as passed at the Delegates conference.
  • Shall maintain Union Finances and submit reports and statements of accounts to the Annual Delegates Conference.
  • Shall approve the establishment of new KUPPET branches.
  • Shall establish gratuity and benevolent funds at National and Branch levels to cater for officers working full time and secretariat staff.
  • Shall ensure 5% of Union income is deposited to manage gratuity scheme for full time officers of the Union.
  • Shall ensure that code of conduct and ethic is developed and enforce strict compliance to the same.
  • Develop, adopt and periodically review a board charter.
  • Develop and recommend for adoption pertinent policies and regulations for effective management of the Union’s affairs.
  • Develop and recommend for approval the annual Budget, annual business plan, proposed branches on the Union and any other annual or quarterly reports.
Articles 8.7.1 - 8.13: Regional Councils, Branches, BGC, BEC & Trustees

8.7.1. Regional Councils

  • There is established 8 Regional Councils consisting of elected officials from respective branches within the region. Regional Councils shall meet at least twice a year to consider union and educational matters within the region.
  • The Regional Council shall in every five years elect its Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary, Assistant Secretary, Treasurer, Vice Treasurer and Women Representative, who shall form the Regional Council Steering Committee.
  • The Regional Councils shall be financed by branch levies and grants as determined by respective Regional Council and approved by the National Executive Board.
  • Every Regional Council shall be registered like any other branch of the union and shall keep proper books of account.
  • There is established 8 Regions in Kenya namely: [i] Coast Region [ii] North Eastern Region [iii] Eastern Region [iv] Central region [v] Rift Valley Region [vi] Western Region [vii] Nyanza Region [viii] Nairobi Region.

8.8.0 The Branch General Assembly (BGA)

  • There shall be branches of the union as shall be established by the National Executive Board on county framework structure of governance.
  • Secretary General shall apply to the Registrar of Trade Unions for the Branch Registration Certificate upon approval by the National Executive Board.
  • The Branch General Assembly shall be convened at least once annually. Shall be convened by the Executive Secretary of the Branch in consultation with the Branch Chairperson, or a requisition of Six (6) members of the Branch Governing Council.
  • The quorum for the Branch General Assembly meetings shall be one third (1/3) of the membership.

Functions of the Branch General Assembly:

  • The Branch shall elect members of both the Branch Governing Council (BCG) and the Branch Executive Committee (BEC).
  • Shall approve financial statements/records at the Branch Annual General Assembly (BGA).
  • Shall have the power to elect branch officials every five years/replace officials through by-elections where/when necessary.
  • Shall run their own financial affairs under the direction of the National Executive Board.

8.9.0 The Branch Governing Council (BGC)

  • The Branch Governing Council shall consist of: The Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Executive Secretary, Assistant Executive Secretary, Treasurer, Assistant Treasurer, Branch Secretary Gender, Branch Secretary Tertiary, Branch Secretary Secondary, and Branch Organising Secretary. A branch shall co-opt committee members to represent various constituencies. A third must comprise the gender balance.
  • The Branch Governing Council shall meet at least once a school term. The quorum for the meeting is one third (1/3).

8.10.0 The Functions of the Branch Governing Council

  • Shall employ branch staff including elected secretary of the branch and any other staff that may be deemed necessary.
  • Shall coordinate Union activities in the Branch.
  • Shall initiate the process of discipline of any member and forward the same to the Secretary General any decision they arrive at their meeting within seven days.
  • Shall maintain union projects at the branch level.
  • Shall solicit; arrange/seek funding for the branch through loans upon approval by the National Executive Board.
  • Shall ensure that they appoint branch auditor to look at the Union accounts at the branch.
  • Shall deposit at least 5% of their branch monthly Union dues as gratuity for full time branch staff.
  • Shall ensure that no member/official publishes views/enter into any deals/moves to court on any matter that commits the union in any way without authorisation from the Secretary General.
  • Shall direct the Assistant Executive Secretary to issue a notice of a meeting in the event the Branch Executive Secretary fails to do so.

8.11.0 Branch Executive Committee (BEC) & 8.12.0 Functions

  • The Branch Executive Committee shall consist of the Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, The Executive Secretary, the Assistant Secretary, the Branch Treasurer, the Branch Assistant Treasurer, Branch Secretary Gender, Branch Secretary Tertiary, Branch Organising Secretary and Branch Secretary Secondary.
  • Shall meet at least once every month.
  • To carry on vigorous mobilisation and campaign and organisational work among teachers in order to help realise the aims and objectives of the Union.
  • To pay relentless attention to the sentiments and remarks of the members of the teaching profession in Kenya and to report the same to the office of the Secretary General.
  • To recruit new members and to supervise the collection of all union fees within their jurisdiction.
  • To keep union records for membership up-to-date and to report any irregularities to the Secretary General.
  • To help organise the teachers within the area where the branch operates and to do everything possible to co-ordinate their efforts, activities and work.
  • To undertake all such activities as may further the aims and objectives of the Union to help execute the policy and programs of the Union and carry out instructions received from Union secretariat at the Head Office.
  • To plan and maintain Union projects in the branch for the teachers on behalf of the National Governing Council.
  • Shall coordinate activities of the Union as spelt out in this constitution and National Governing Council during strikes and lock-outs.
  • Nominate three (3) women representatives in their first meeting after election.

8.13.0 Trustees

  • There shall be Nine (9) Trustees, eight of whom shall be elected at the Delegates’ Conference every five years reflecting regional balance. The ninth slot shall be occupied by the founder Secretary General of the union, until the occupant is rendered ineligible.
  • The election of Trustees shall be organised in such a manner that four of the eight elective Trustees shall at all times be officials of opposite gender.
  • The Trustees shall hold office for at least five years and shall jointly be vested with the custody of the union’s property and bank accounts.
  • The Trustees shall oversee handing over and taking over from the preceding to succeeding officers within seven (7) days upon the registration of new officers.
  • The Trustees shall hold meetings at least once every school term and shall be facilitated by the Executive Board.
  • A Trustee may be removed from office at the Annual Delegates Conference/ or may voluntarily resign in writing addressed to the Secretary General.
  • The Trustees shall have the authority to scrutinise the books of accounts of a branch/national office and recommend to the National Governing Council and /or Annual Delegates’ Conference (ADC/SDC) the cause of action to be taken.
  • The Trustees shall keep inventory of union property at the Branches and National Office.
Articles 9.0 - 11.0: Notice for Meetings & Duties of Office Bearers

ARTICLE 9.0: NOTICE FOR MEETINGS

All union meetings shall be convened through the issuance of a notice by the secretary general for Annual Delegates Conferences/Special Conferences, National Governing Council meetings, National Executive Board Meetings and Union committee meetings. The Executive Secretary shall issue notices of meetings related to county branch structures. However, any branch organ may requisition and summon such a meeting by a resolution assented to by at least 6 members of that Branch organ in case the Executive secretary fails to call a meeting.

ARTICLE 10.0: OFFICE BEARERS

NATIONAL OFFICE: National Chairperson, National Vice Chairperson, Secretary General, Deputy Secretary General, National Secretary Gender, National Treasurer, Assistant National Treasurer, National Secretary Tertiary, National Secretary secondary, National Organising Secretary, 1ST Assistant National Gender Secretary, 2ND Assistant National Gender Secretary, 3RD Assistant Gender Secretary and Assistant National Secretary Junior Secondary.

BRANCH OFFICES: Branch Chairperson, Branch Vice Chairperson, Branch Executive Secretary, Branch Assistant Executive Secretary, Branch Treasurer, Branch Assistant Treasurer, Branch Secretary Gender, Branch Secretary Tertiary, Branch Secretary Secondary and Branch Organising Secretary, 1ST Assistant Gender Secretary, 2ND Assistant Gender Secretary, 3RD Assistant Gender Secretary, and Assistant Secretary Junior Secondary.

ARTICLE 11.0: DUTIES OF OFFICE BEARERS

  • National Chairperson: Preside at all conferences/meetings/forums except branch meetings; lead all delegations; enforce constitution observance; cast a deciding vote in case of ties.
  • National Vice Chairperson: Act in the absence of the National Chairperson and perform delegated duties.
  • Secretary General: Chief Executive Officer and spokesperson; execute and communicate board and conference strategies; prepare agendas and minutes; maintain membership register; custodian of the seal; authorize expenditures and budgetary controls.
  • Deputy Secretary General: Assist the SG, act in their absence, and perform assigned constitutional duties.
  • National Treasurer: Accountable for Union funds; maintain books of accounts; prepare financial reports for NGC and ADC; disburse monthly dues to branches (65%); allocate gratuity funds (5%).
  • Assistant National Treasurer: Assist the Treasurer and act in their absence.
  • National Secretary Gender / Tertiary / Secondary / Organising Secretary / Assistants: Coordinate respective gender, institutional, and operational activities across the union and secretariat.
  • Branch Office Bearers (Chairperson, Executive Secretary, Treasurer, etc.): Perform corresponding leadership, administrative, financial, and organizational duties at the county branch level.
Articles 12.0 - 17.0: Term of Office, Elections, By-Elections, Fees & Funds

ARTICLE 12.0: TERM OF OFFICE BEARERS

All office-bearers shall be elected to serve for a term of five (5) years, but eligible for re-election. The retirement age of the union officials shall be Sixty-Five (65) years.

ARTICLE 13.0: NATIONAL AND BRANCH ELECTIONS & BY-ELECTIONS

  • Elections shall be held every five years by secret ballot at special delegates conferences (national) and Branch General Assembly (branches).
  • Candidates for national office must be delegates and resign from branch positions 31 days prior.
  • Supervised by accredited government officers from the Ministry of Labour in accordance with the Labour Relations Act.
  • By-Elections: Held in case of transfer, promotion, death, resignation, absence abroad >6 months, criminal conviction, court removal, or NGC expulsion. Assistants act in the interim.

ARTICLE 14.0: NOMINATION FEES

  • Secretary General / National Chairman / National Treasurer: Kshs. 500,000 each
  • Deputy SG / National Vice Chairman / Org Secretary / Gender & Sector Secretaries: Kshs. 300,000 each
  • Executive Secretary: Kshs. 200,000
  • Branch Chairman / Treasurer: Kshs. 100,000 each
  • Vice Chairman / Assistant Secretary / Vice Treasurer / Org Secretary / Gender / Sector Secretaries at Branch: Kshs. 50,000 each

ARTICLE 15.0: COMMITTEES

Includes the Finance Committee (max 7 members), Professional and Standards Committee (max 5 members), and Constitutional and By-laws Committee (max 5 members) appointed by the NGC.

ARTICLE 16.0: GRATUITY

National and Branch Governing Councils shall formulate by-laws to regulate gratuity schemes. 5% of Union monthly income shall be deposited to an account specifically created to facilitate gratuity for qualified registered officials at the end of their 5-year term.

ARTICLE 17.0: UNION FUNDS

Maintained in bank accounts approved by the union. Accounts close on December 31st annually and are audited by qualified accountants by March 31st. Branches receive 65% of total monthly dues subject to statutory and operational variations.

Articles 18.0 - 27.0: Auditors, Accounts, Discipline, Industrial Action, Dissolution & Signatories

ARTICLE 18.0: AUDITORS

Delegates’ Conference shall appoint qualified Accountants to audit union accounts annually, with reports displayed at registered offices.

ARTICLE 19.0: INSPECTION OF AUDITED ACCOUNTS

Paid-up members have the right to inspect certified books of accounts, which shall also be available on the website.

ARTICLE 20.0: RULES OF PROCEDURE & MEETINGS

Quorum must be declared before meetings. Resolutions are passed by single majority. Minutes signed by the Chairperson form conclusive evidence.

ARTICLE 21.0: DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE & OFFENCES

  • Offences: Misuse of union funds, negligence, bribery, sexual harassment, economic crimes, and gross violation of public policy or union rules.
  • Sanctions: Caution, warning, surcharge/fine, suspension, expulsion, branch deregistration, or judicial prosecution. Suspension/expulsion requires a 2/3 NGC resolution.

ARTICLE 22.0: INDUSTRIAL ACTION / DISPUTE

No cessation of work or strikes shall take place without direct sanctioning by the National Governing Council. Members shall report grievances to the Branch Executive Secretary for escalation to the Secretary General.

ARTICLE 23.0 - 25.0: BY-LAWS, INTERPRETATION & AMENDMENTS

Constitutional alterations require a 2/3 majority vote at the Annual or Special Delegates’ Conference with notices submitted 6 months in advance.

ARTICLE 26.0: DISSOLUTION OF THE UNION

Authorized exclusively by the Delegates’ Conference with a 6-month notice and a 2/3 majority vote of votes cast. Remaining assets are transferred to a judicial body in accordance with the Constitution of Kenya.
